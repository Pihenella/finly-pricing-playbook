Supplier memo

English version for China

Блок для китайского поставщика: без внутренней детализации, но с понятной логикой долгой программы.

Cooperation Intent

We are building a long-term supply program, not a one-time test order. Our first China order is 520 pcs / 1,998,270 RUB equivalent. Due to customs delay, the first shipment is expected to arrive in late July.

To avoid stock-outs before the China orders arrive, we will use a temporary local supplier for part of the volume. This local volume replaces part of later China orders; it does not increase total overstock.

What We Need

  • Priority production for fast-moving items.
  • Confirmed lead time for the July repeat order.
  • Stable pricing until the end of 2026.
  • QC photos and videos before balance payment.
  • Packaging and labels prepared for Wildberries FBO.
  • Weekly production and shipment status updates.
